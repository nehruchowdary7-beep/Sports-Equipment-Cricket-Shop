Frequently Asked Questions

What is included with your Free Machine Bat Knocking? +

Every bat eligible for free knocking receives 15,000 automated pneumatic strokes calibrated from toe to sweet spot, 2 coats of raw linseed oil, anti-scuff sheet application, and heavy-duty fiber edge tape.

How do you guarantee authenticity against counterfeits? +

SportX only sources direct from certified master factories (SS Sunridges, SG, MRF, Yonex Japan, Nike, Puma). Every item ships with its tamper-evident manufacturer serial hologram and unique scannable verification seal.

What is your shipping speed and return policy? +

All domestic orders over $150 qualify for Free Express Shipping (2-3 business days). Unused equipment in original packaging with intact security seals may be returned within 30 days for a full refund.

Can I try out bats in your San Francisco showroom? +

Yes! Our showroom features an indoor practice bay equipped with high-speed cameras and bat sensors so you can test weight pickup, swing speed, and balance before making your choice.
