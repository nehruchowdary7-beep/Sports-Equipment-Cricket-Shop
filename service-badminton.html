Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the Badminton Club Pack?

The club pack includes ultra-light tournament polos, breathable training tees, moisture-wicking shorts with ball pockets, 12-racket squad thermal bags, and tournament towel grips.

Do you offer team racket restringing alongside uniforms?

Yes, our electronic workshop strings entire squad rosters using Yonex BG80, Aerobite, or Victor strings calibrated to exact requested tensions with next-day turnaround.

Are country flags and player surnames supported for tournament play?

Yes, full BWF-compliant rear lettering including player surname, national association code, and junior club crest is included with zero setup fees.

What is the recommended wash routine for ultra-light badminton fabrics?

Cold machine wash at 30°C with mild liquid detergent. The micro-knit structure air dries in under 25 minutes without requiring ironing or tumble drying.
