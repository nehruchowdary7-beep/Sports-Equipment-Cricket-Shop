TECHNICAL CARE PROTOCOLS • MAXIMIZE LIFESPAN

Equipment Longevity & Maintenance Protocol

01

English Willow Protocol

  • Moisture Balance: Maintain 10-12% moisture. Avoid storing in hot car boots or damp garages.
  • Raw Linseed Oiling: Apply a light coat every 8-10 weeks to prevent cleft brittleness.
  • Toe Guard Shield: Replace rubberized toe guards annually to prevent damp moisture creep.
Average Lifespan: 3-5 Competitive Seasons
02

Carbon Racket Protocol

  • Tension Decay Cycles: Restring every 30-40 playing hours to prevent dead frame stress.
  • Thermal Guard: Thermal insulation racket bag is essential to avoid resin micro-fractures.
  • Grommet Inspection: Replace split grommets immediately during stringing to protect frame.
Average Lifespan: 200+ Match Hours
03

Footwear & Steel Care

  • Cleat Air Drying: Remove insoles and dry naturally at room temp. Never use radiators.
  • Stud Thread Lube: Apply silicon grease to screw-in studs to prevent corrosion seizing.
  • Olympic Knurl Brushing: Nylon brush chalk out of barbell knurling after heavy sessions.
Average Lifespan: Lifetime Steel / 12-18 Cleats
